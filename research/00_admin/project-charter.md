# Project Charter

## Objective

Build and evaluate a professor-configurable pedagogical Digital Twin platform
that supports multiple professors and courses, answers with approved evidence,
teaches according to explicit professor policy, and requires evaluation before
student publication.

## Scope

- Instructor onboarding and teaching-policy capture
- Multi-course source governance and evidence-complete retrieval
- Professor-controlled preview, evaluation, publication, withdrawal, and rollback
- Invite-only professor, student, and administrator workflows
- Persistent, course-isolated student tutoring with citations
- Professor-fidelity, pedagogy, retrieval, reliability, and capacity evaluation
- Local deployment with a documented hosting path

## Claim boundary

- No human usability, adoption, engagement, or learning-effectiveness claims
- No public signup, institutional SSO, or required LMS integration
- No automatic grading or assessed-work completion
- No solution files, answer keys, student submissions, or student data
- No universal state-of-the-art or production service-level claim
