# Persona-robust autonomy release selection 022

## Result

The network-free actual-product development comparison completed with a
`Keep` decision. T1-v2 autonomous is the candidate for one fresh
provider-backed confirmation; it is not yet promoted to the release profile.

- 108 paired 30-day histories per primary condition.
- 18 balanced T1-v2 reactive ablation histories.
- Six learner personas, three public-wording methods, two independently
  specified transition families, and three seeds.
- Zero observable hard-safety failures across all 234 histories.
- Mean final hidden-mastery proxy: 0.3337 for T1-v2 autonomous versus 0.2839
  for T0; paired difference +0.0498 (95% bootstrap CI +0.0351 to +0.0658).
- Worst-persona mean: 0.1839 for T1-v2 autonomous versus 0.1461 for T0.
- T1-v2 autonomous attribution accuracy: 96.40%.

## Important limitation

T1-v2 autonomous delivered 10.70 messages per 30-day history on average and
39.68% of interventions were classified as wasted by hidden simulator truth.
The condition still ranked first under the frozen safety-first lexicographic
rule because it passed every hard gate and improved the worst-persona and
overall learning proxies. The wasted-intervention rate must remain visible in
the fresh confirmation and release decision.

This is simulated-learner development evidence. It does not establish real
student learning improvement, usability, professor fidelity, or true-visual
quality. Frozen GPT-5.4 nano wording changed public utterance form only;
deterministic hidden learner truth remained authoritative.

## Next decision

Freeze T1-v2 autonomous for one fresh provider-backed confirmation. A valid
pass may open the winner-only known 10,000+1,000 regression and local HTTPS
qualification. A valid quality failure ends in `No Release` rather than
another prompt-tuning loop.
