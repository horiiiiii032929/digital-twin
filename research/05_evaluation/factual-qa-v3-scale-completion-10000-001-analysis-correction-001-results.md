# Factual-QA cumulative 10,000-case interpretation correction 001

Result ID:
`factual-qa-v3-scale-completion-10000-001-analysis-correction-001`

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: Complete retrospective analysis; no dataset, provider, or model rerun

Decision: **Keep the historical run as engineering pipeline evidence, but do
not use it as academic evidence of Digital Twin factual-QA performance. Go
Deeper with a new end-to-end evaluation.**

## Why the interpretation changes

The historical run executed 10,000 rows successfully, but row count alone does
not establish 10,000 independent factual-QA tests.

- The synthetic corpus contains 1,000 source units across 20 courses, generated
  from eight fixed claim grammars.
- Canonical questions and answers are generated deterministically from those
  templates.
- The author model receives the expected action, canonical question, canonical
  answer, target claims, and boundary reason. Its only task is to produce a
  question variant.
- The final case assembler copies the answer, action, claims, and citations from
  the authoritative truth package. The Digital Twin does not independently
  retrieve evidence or generate an answer in this run.
- The validation reported 35 near-duplicate signature groups, with the largest
  containing 680 rows. These correlated template families make row-level
  success rates look more precise than the underlying content diversity
  supports.
- The 12-case Codex priority review checked consistency with generated metadata;
  it was not independent expert annotation.

## Claims that remain valid

The original run remains useful evidence that the bounded generation and review
pipeline can:

- process 10,000 deterministic synthetic truth packages at the recorded cost
  and latency;
- preserve action, claim, source, citation, and boundary metadata;
- enforce deterministic citation and mutation checks;
- journal and resume a large provider workflow; and
- expose advisory-reviewer disagreements and malformed responses.

Its original files, hashes, call accounting, metrics, and `Keep` decision are
preserved as the decision made at that time.

## Claims this run cannot support

This result does not estimate:

- factual correctness of answers generated by the actual Digital Twin;
- retrieval recall or citation grounding when gold evidence is not injected;
- performance on realistic or independently varied course content;
- generalization to new sources, question families, or multimodal material; or
- Professor Digital Twin fidelity, tutoring quality, or production readiness.

Multi-model agreement is advisory and is not ground truth.

## Corrected decision boundary

Issue #110 remains complete only as the synthetic pipeline-scale engineering
milestone. Academic factual-QA validation moves to a separate prospective
evaluation in which the frozen system sees only a question and the indexed
corpus. Gold answers, claims, source spans, and citations are withheld from the
system and used only by the evaluator.

The successor protocol is
[`research/04_experiments/2026-08-25-academic-factual-qa-end-to-end-plan.md`](../04_experiments/2026-08-25-academic-factual-qa-end-to-end-plan.md).
