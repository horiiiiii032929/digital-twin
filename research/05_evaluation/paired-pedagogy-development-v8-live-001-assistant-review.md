# V8 meaningful continuation development review

All 168 delivered turns from 96 histories were inspected against the unchanged rubric, including preceding turns and secondary-concept disclosure. This is assistant-authored, unblinded review of reused synthetic development data, with root adjudication; it is not human validation or a population accuracy estimate.

| Measure | V4 | V8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Useful targets /48 | 16 | 39 |
| Primary targets /16 | 2 | 16 |
| Verified critical disclosure events | 2 | 0 |

V8 meets every narrow main gate: 39/48 overall, 16/16 primary, 14 primary paired wins and two ties, with a definite improvement in each primary form. Across all contexts there are 26 wins, three losses and 19 ties. All 108 calls per arm completed, but V8 delivered one local guarded failure on a preceding turn. A successful provider response is not sufficient for useful tutoring.

Six failures remain in the explanatory profile: three compound-source and three mixed-evidence requests receive quizzes instead of the requested explanations. The other failures are two unresolved-reference responses and one Kestrel topic-switch response that falsely describes the available recovery rule as absent. Root adjudication retains that last defect despite its useful initial question. The explanation-first slice itself is 4/4; this does not erase failures in other explanatory-profile contexts or establish the broader 95% completion criterion.

Control case32 stage3 and case39 stage2 disclose the complete Kestrel recovery restore-and-recheck rule before any recovery attempt. Earlier relay attempts do not satisfy the explicit new-concept requirement. Both control targets were already inadequate, so their classification does not reduce 16/48 further. The candidate's related initial prompts provide only partial conditions or alternatives, not the complete secondary solution.

**Decision:** Keep the narrow development gate result; Refine broader profile alignment. Passing this gate does not make the system complete, validate professor fidelity, demonstrate student learning, or consume the closed confirmation packet. Four fictional courses provide few independent clusters; paired counts are descriptive and do not support a generalizable accuracy confidence interval.

Per-axis judgments, all delivered texts, actual citation metadata, raw hashes and source-identity checks are retained in the [machine-readable review](paired-pedagogy-development-v8-live-001-assistant-review.json). Whole-chunk source association is not entailment verification. The [frozen rubric](meaningful-continuation-rubric-v1.md) was not altered after outputs.
