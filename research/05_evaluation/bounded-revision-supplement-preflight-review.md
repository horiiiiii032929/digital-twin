license disclosure of the newly requested mechanism. The scenarios vary
conjunction, ordered actions, thresholds, arithmetic updates, exceptions,
priority and membership. Cairn's “only when” clause establishes a necessary
condition, not an equivalence: subsequent review must preserve that direction.

explicit initial-withholding requirement. Specific starting cues alone are
not full-solution disclosure.
