## Decision question and configuration

The question was whether the direct OpenAI R1 path could be made into one
finite, leakage-resistant, interruption-safe checkpoint without importing any
historical Gemini, DeepSeek, or Mistral reviewer votes.

- Wording and T0 generation: `gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17`.
- Advisory semantic review: `gpt-5.4-2026-03-05`.
- API: direct OpenAI Responses API with strict structured outputs and
  `store: false`.
- Calibration: 20 clean and 20 deliberately corrupted immutable controls in
  ten four-case calls; expected labels remain hidden until all votes persist.
- Maximum: 660 calls, zero retries, and separate ceilings of USD 3, USD 5,
  USD 8, and USD 2 for calibration, wording, candidate, and control stages.
- Deterministic source-linked truth remains authoritative; GPT-5.4 is advisory.

The model snapshots, pricing, limits, and data controls were refreshed from
official OpenAI documentation on 2026-08-28. Paid execution still requires a
fresh no-call preflight and separate authorization.
