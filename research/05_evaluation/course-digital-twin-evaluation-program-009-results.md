# Course Digital Twin evaluation program 009 result

## Outcome

Program 009 is preserved as `Refine` development evidence and an interrupted
final-construction execution. The descriptive 500-case candidate achieved
59.75% fully grounded factual success, 93.40% overall action accuracy, 100%
boundary action accuracy, and zero severe unsupported releases. It failed the
frozen factual, claim, citation, completion, version-validity, and paired-
retention gates. The 100-case any-hit control achieved 73.75% fully grounded
success; the candidate's paired supported-answer retention delta was -18.75
percentage points, with a -31.94-point lower 95% bound.

This is a genuine quality result. It is not erased by the later operational
correction.

## Operational interruption

Final 10,000-case construction was stopped after 37 wording batches and 36
independent-verification batches. Live usage showed that the strong GPT-5.4
verifier would cost approximately USD 14–15, while the stage reserved USD 5.
Continuing would have produced a predictable budget stop around 18% completion.

The interrupted construction used USD 1.07016455. Together with retrieval and
development, program 009 used 610 completed provider calls and USD 2.57004316.
No final package was created, no final product response was executed, and no
final hidden gold was opened.

## Decision

Terminate and revoke program 009. Program 010 changes only operational
execution: GPT-5.4 mini performs bulk question verification, four course streams
may progress concurrently while preserving within-course order, and the
remaining authorized budget is reallocated from measured usage. Questions,
canonical truth, scoring gates, the failed development result, and the
descriptive status remain unchanged.

## Limitations

- The 500 development cases are now known and cannot support a fresh
  confirmatory claim.
- Program 010's final run is descriptive because both retrieval and product
  development failed frozen gates.
- Model-assisted checks use one provider family and remain advisory.
- There is no independent external human annotation, professor-fidelity,
  usability, learning-outcome, or production-hosting claim.
