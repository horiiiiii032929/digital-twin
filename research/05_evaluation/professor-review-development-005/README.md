# Professor review packet: development examples

Purpose: obtain instructor feedback on the evaluation rubric and teaching
preferences before preparing independent confirmation. **No review or approval
has been obtained.** Nothing has been sent to an instructor or participant.

## What to review

1. Read [eight responses in four situations](review-packet.md). They are actual
   saved outputs from development run005, with implementation labels replaced
   by review labels. The underlying course facts are fictional.
2. Use the [blank reviewer template](reviewer-template.csv). For each dimension,
   enter `pass`, `fail`, `uncertain`, or `not applicable` and a brief reason. Do
   not assign a positive result solely because a quotation matches the source.
3. Check whether the response answers the requested question, follows the
   stated preference, reveals too much, and makes appropriate use of prior turns.
   For a question, explain whether it would help the learner take a next step.
4. Flag ambiguous source/question pairs. State which behavior you would prefer
   and why; ties and uncertainty are legitimate outcomes.

The [allocation key](allocation-key.json) is for the researcher after initial
review. It is not part of the initial reviewer packet. Identity masking here
does not establish a blinded study: the student/Codex selected these examples,
and their style can reveal their condition. Preserve the original labels,
responses and any unfavorable comments.

Review dimensions follow the
[qualitative rubric](../cross-course-quality-assistant-review-rubric-v1.md).
These examples have no genuine student solution attempts, so they cannot
validate progression after a correct or incorrect attempt. A separate
[progression rubric](../dialogue-progression-assistant-review-rubric-v1.md)
addresses that question. No grades, approval or learning log should be inferred
from an empty template.

## Material and permissions needed next

The following is a preparation checklist, not a request already sent to anyone:

- Identify the course and versions of representative materials that may be
  processed: selectable PDF slides/notes, text lecture transcripts and, if
  permitted, deidentified instructor forum exchanges.
- Record who can approve each source and the separate permissions to process,
  use for tutoring, display excerpts, and send content to the intended external
  model provider. Confirm ownership/licensing for public materials as well.
- Exclude student identifiers, private conversations, graded submissions and
  confidential material unless explicitly authorized under an appropriate
  protocol. Deidentification attestation is not automatic anonymization.
- Provide instructor-approved teaching preferences and a small set of example
  exchanges illustrating Socratic/explanatory style, misconception correction,
  hint progression, refusal and escalation. Record permission to use examples
  for prompting, development, review and quotation in the report separately.
- Reserve distinct examples/questions for independent review and confirmation;
  do not place those same examples into prompts or tune on their labels.
- Review at least20% of planned quality labels independently, recording reviewer
  role, disagreement and corrections before confirmation. These eight selected
  development responses do **not** satisfy that percentage automatically.

The research quality plan calls for roughly four heterogeneous permitted
courses. One instructor's complete UI journey plus a second course for isolation
does not replace that research requirement. If representative materials or
independent review are unavailable, report the narrower synthetic scope.

## What this feedback could and could not support

It could improve the rubric, clarify the instructor's intended behavior and
identify weaknesses worth correcting. It cannot establish real-student learning,
representative usability, faithful instructor identity replication, scalability
or a final quality rate. Because all examples were previously consumed in
development, favorable review does not turn them into fresh held-out evidence.
Final instructor-fidelity claims require permitted real-instructor references
and review of responses not used to configure or tune the tutor.
