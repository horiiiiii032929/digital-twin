# Full operating simulation: bounded qualitative review

Run: `final-profile-operational-dialogue-development-001-full-live-001`.
Decision: **Keep the operating evidence; Refine tutoring and outreach quality.** Thirty simulated days of successful persistence and delivery do not establish useful teaching, real learning, or professor fidelity.

This is an unblinded assistant review, using the [existing progression rubric](dialogue-progression-assistant-review-rubric-v1.md). Selection was defined **after execution**: all 13 `safe-graph-failure` turns, all 16 proactive messages, and the first and last non-guard-failure tutor turn from each of 24 histories (48 endpoint turns). The [77 sanitized per-case findings](dialogue-full-live-001-assistant-findings.json) retain actual text, nearby context, file references, and review-time source hashes. These deliberately selected events are not a random quality sample, and no semantic accuracy estimate or confidence interval is inferred.

## Findings

- **Closed failures:** Eleven responses were incomplete at the 500-output-token limit. Two further schema-valid proposals copied “what is your current explanation” from prior tutor text into `question_focus`, instead of selecting text from the current student question. The exact-focus guard rejected those proposals: misconception-prone/Socratic/reactive, day 8 (provider attempt 250), and low-receptivity/Socratic/reactive, day 9 (attempt 487). All 13 delivered a nonfactual failure message rather than helpful tutoring. Defensive validation succeeded; assistance still failed.
- **Initial profile behavior:** The first endpoint in each of the 12 Socratic histories elicited an attempt. Three of the 12 explanatory histories also asked the same generic question, instead of the approved initial explanation: low-receptivity autonomous and reactive, and slow-learner reactive. These are selected finite observations, not a general profile-fidelity rate.
- **Late behavior:** Most final endpoints supplied the relevant approved rule, often as two sentence fragments. Responses to correct attempts commonly repeated what the learner had already supplied without acknowledgement, a worked application, or a new diagnostic step. In inspected surrounding context, correct attempts sometimes elicited another generic request for the same explanation. The final misconception-prone/Socratic/autonomous response gave a relevant partial probe hint; its reactive counterpart reached the full lantern rule after a hint. These are useful local signs of progression, with no demonstrated mastery or overall learning effect.
- **Outreach:** Every one of the 16 proactive messages quoted an entire approved rule and added a generic application or contrast instruction. The quoted facts match the synthetic cards and have actual source/region citation metadata. No unsupported course fact was identified in this selected outreach set. However, the wrapper does not identify a concrete activity or the learner's actual alternative, and identical reminders recur on consecutive days. Fourteen of the 16 messages follow a latest same-concept attempt on an earlier day that already reproduces the correct source rule. This is evidence that outreach need and personalization remain unqualified; a correct synthetic utterance is itself not proof of mastery or absence of a learning need.

Outreach contains full explanations, including in Socratic histories. In these selected histories prior attempts exist, so full quotation alone does not establish a violation of an attempt-before-answer rule. The full run has no never-direct-answer arm; it cannot qualify that stronger restriction for proactive delivery. A reflective opening phrase does not make a full quoted solution a partial hint.

## Interpretation and next decision

The source-backed explanations establish basic factual delivery for these small protocol cards. The observed weaknesses concern response adequacy after an attempt, inappropriate repetition, rigid failure recovery, and the usefulness of unsolicited support. Earlier finite Luna/Terra comparison already found similar limitations. Extending the simulated timeline has not resolved them.

A next quality candidate should address these behaviors with a fresh prospectively defined comparison: correct and incorrect application attempts, explicit non-attempts, repeated requests after a guarded failure, and outreach following successful work or a topic change. Review response usefulness separately from source membership and delivered-message counts. Preserve this run and its negative findings; do not promote quality from operational completion.

The simulation used synthetic students, fixed authored course rules, and an artificial time advance. Persona names such as “fast learner” or “high forgetting” describe driver configuration, not measured student traits. There was no independent human review or measured learning outcome. Tutor context in this sidecar consists of the preceding two turns; proactive context conservatively uses strictly earlier simulated days to avoid treating same-day responses as prior evidence. The latest same-concept attempt check also excludes the outreach day. This review does not audit every intervening tutor answer, all internal trigger reasoning, or deployed authentication and scaling.
