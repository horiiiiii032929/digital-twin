# Cross-engine evaluation 010 network-free harness result

Decision: **Keep the independent scoring and finite-program harness; Go Deeper on product quality.**

The actual product path completed all 820 public-synthetic autonomy cases under
the deterministic E0 engine. Cases traversed the tutoring service, SQLite
persistence, governed graph, proactive worker, outbox, delivery, virtual-time,
and restart paths. The independent scorer derived its decisions from raw
action, authority, citation, state, delivery, and restart evidence rather than
from the product's own pass/fail flags.

All 820 cases passed the independent hard gates. Action accuracy,
event-to-action eligibility, authority preservation, citation lineage,
state/action/delivery reconciliation, restart consistency, pedagogical
transition validity, goal termination, and the primary
`safe-grounded-autonomous-success` rate were each 100%.

## What this establishes

- The flow-independent scorer can detect product defects without trusting
  self-reported graph invariants.
- The 820-case realistic-time portfolio is executable through the real product
  services and remains finite and restart-aware.
- The six-engine program contract fixes the evaluation method while changing
  only the planner/generator binding.
- The SQLite program ledger enforces ordered stages, immutable resume bindings,
  unique case records, cost accounting, and terminal quality stops.

## What this does not establish

- No provider-backed engine was called and no LLM product-quality claim is made.
- E0's perfect result is a deterministic harness control, not evidence that an
  LLM-backed autonomous tutor is release-ready.
- The sealed 1,000 cases and known 10,000+1,000 regression remain unopened.
- Professor fidelity, real-student usability, and real learning improvement are
  not established; their later LLM-only tracks are explicitly proxy evidence.

Evaluation 009 remains immutable historical development evidence. Program 010
supersedes it before paid execution and must preserve the same cases, retrieval,
policy, prompts, clocks, seeds, and scorer across engines.
