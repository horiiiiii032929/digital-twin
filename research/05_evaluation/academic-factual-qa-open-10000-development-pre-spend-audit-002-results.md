# Corrected open-benchmark development reference audit

## Outcome

The prospective complete-region reference layer passes its frozen network-free
gate. Keep this reference method and proceed to provider-neutral question
wording before product execution. This result does **not** evaluate T0 answer
quality and does not authorize the 500-case or final 10,000-case runs.

## Method

The successor planner identifies complete semantic regions before constructing
source clusters. Text targets use complete statements. Structured targets bind
to full detected code, equation, or table regions, while short canonical claims
remain linked to those original regions. The planner then creates 100
non-overlapping development clusters—25 per course—and code deterministically
builds separate public inputs and hidden gold.

The audit checked package separation, uniqueness, answer leakage, exact source
quotes and ranges, complete statements, structured-region containment,
boundary-empty lineage, and byte stability. No model or provider participated
in these authoritative checks.

## Results

- 100 source clusters and 500 cases: 400 answerable and 100 boundary.
- Cluster allocation: 56 text, 35 code, 6 equation, and 3 table.
- 224/224 text targets passed complete-statement and exact-lineage checks.
- 176/176 structured targets passed original-region lineage and modality checks.
- Zero reference defects, normalized duplicates, canonical-answer leaks, public
  gold fields, provider calls, private-data reads, or final-split access.
- The fixed 100-case paired control remains a complete 20-cluster subset.

## Codex-assisted semantic packet

usable source truth and correct action/lineage structure. Ten had serviceable
development wording; two exposed expected template-language limitations

not independent human annotation. Consequently, the reference layer is **Keep**
while wording remains **Go Deeper** through the provider-neutral wording

duplicate, or answer-leaking output falls back to the canonical question.
