# Main dialogue source–oracle inconsistency

**Refine the instrument; do not qualify a model from the affected main aggregate.**
The V14-medium Stage B review found that the v2 main packet mixes a necessary
condition in source text with a complete decision procedure in its gold meanings.
This affects the historical v2 main scores as factual qualification evidence,
including earlier V10/V11 summaries. Preserve all original inputs, outputs and
ratings; append this limitation rather than overwriting unfavorable records.
Component revision controls, the separate short logic packet and profile/boundary/
mixed packets are not invalidated by this finding.

## Counterexample

Topaz source: “Topaz voucher redeems a purchase only when available credits are
at least the purchase cost.” It separately describes subtracting cost *on*
redemption and rejecting insufficient credits. Gold for development-04-03,
04-05 and04-06 demands redemption and5 remaining credits for credits9/cost4.

Let A mean sufficient credits and B mean redemption. The source gives B⇒A,
not A⇒B. A=true, B=false satisfies that rule and the insufficient-credit clause.
The conditional subtraction clause is also satisfied because redemption did not
occur. Thus the source does not entail the demanded completed transaction.
This is an oracle defect, not a legitimate basis for penalizing an answer that
correctly distinguishes eligibility from execution.

The primary Kestrel, Marble and Willow sources use the same only-when form.
Their negative applications are supported, but unconditional positive paraphrases
remain sensitive to this ambiguity. For example, V14-medium trial1 Marble
repeated-stuck stage2 says “If it is, the store accepts the incoming version.”
Trial1 Willow explanation/mixed responses omit “only”; Topaz responses frequently
assert sufficient credits guarantee redemption. Under the literal constraints,
these are unsupported strengthening. Under the gold's intended total decision
procedure, they are expected behavior. Neither interpretation may be silently
chosen to obtain a passing aggregate.

All delivered main turns in both V14-medium trials were inspected. Trial2
Kestrel stuck-application stage1 explicitly notes that a matching stamp is
necessary and does not establish that every matching item is forwarded. This
illustrates why oracle consistency matters. No all-clear critical count or
factual main accuracy is claimed for these trials. The executions remain useful
for plumbing, source/usage accounting and descriptive dialogue inspection.

## Correction boundary

Create a separately versioned complete-procedure packet whose four primary
sources state explicit if/otherwise behavior; synchronize quoted learner attempts
and source-version/span bindings. Preserve all48 contexts, profiles, stages and
thresholds. Retain the original sources in an additional eight-case necessary-
condition diagnostic with logically correct gold, so clarification does not
remove the challenge of distinguishing necessity from sufficiency. Preregister
both reruns before outputs. Runtime, prompts and model configuration remain fixed.

Review is by the root coding assistant, not independent human validation. The
truth-table counterexample is inspectable and does not rely on a model rating.
