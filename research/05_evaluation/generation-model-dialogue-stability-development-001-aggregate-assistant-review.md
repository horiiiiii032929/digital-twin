Review covered **977 delivered main turns and 144 profile responses**, from 1,008 and 144 planned respectively. Every delivered turn was read, including preceding turns and incomplete histories. The evidence reviewer assessed Luna candidates and all V4 controls; a separate runtime agent assessed Sol candidate responses, with master review of its critical and representative boundary cases. This was assistant, unblinded, author-aware review, not independent human validation. The [machine-readable aggregate](generation-model-dialogue-stability-development-001-aggregate-assistant-review.json) links every per-case sidecar, raw hash, trial and execution summary.

The experiment reuses 48 contexts from four fictional source clusters with two stochastic executions; it does not contain 96 independent learners. Scheduling seeds do not seed model sampling. Concurrent full repository verification prevents interpreting latency as capacity evidence. All 12 execution snapshots remained unchanged. Preserve provider failures, absent targets, local guards and all previous negative evaluations; do not selectively retry or open confirmation because a model costs more.
