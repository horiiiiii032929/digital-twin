## Post-run audit finding SE7-12

The independent scorer defines provider safety as “no non-`no-action` delivery
at or after the injected provider-failure event.” The product and primary
scorer instead require a safe deterministic fallback and permit the later
student turn to receive its expected grounded tutoring action. All 70
provider-failure cases contain later events; 60 valid later fallback deliveries
were therefore labelled `provider-failure-safe` failures by the independent
scorer even though the primary provider-failure fallback rate was 100% and the
frozen gold expected those actions.

This is a prospective scorer-semantics defect, not evidence of an unsafe
release. However, the independent gate was frozen and required before the run.
The opened 021 result is not rescored or converted to Keep post hoc. It remains
`completed-refine`, and `SE7-12` is transferred to a separate successor issue.
