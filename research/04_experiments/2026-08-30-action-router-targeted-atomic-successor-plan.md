# Action-router and targeted-atomic successor

## Decision question

Can one method-level correction—deterministic boundary routing plus
question-targeted atomic evidence and answer construction—correct the valid
AFQC-109 T0 product failure without changing the selected atomic retriever?

## Prediction and comparison

The candidate should eliminate the five unsafe ambiguity releases and reduce
source-supported but off-target claims. The control retains the same retriever,
OpenAI model, policy, corpus, and scoring but uses the historical structured
gate and extractive generator. This is a method-level comparison because the
candidate intentionally changes routing, evidence selection, and answer
construction together.

## Fresh data and limitations

The confirmation contains 100 clusters, 500 candidate cases, and a frozen
100-case paired control. Every selected character range is disjoint from the
historical 2,100-cluster plan, AFQC-103, and atomic-M2 development. The four
pinned repositories cannot support another 100-cluster portfolio that is also
fully source-family-disjoint. Family overlap is therefore disclosed and any
uncertainty must be clustered by source family rather than treating rows as
independent.

The sealed 10,000 cases remain unopened. Public questions, hidden gold,
responses, provider ledgers, and scoring are separate. Deterministic source,
action, claim, citation, and policy checks remain authoritative.

## Finite boundary

The run permits at most 20 embedding calls and 600 product calls, zero retries,
and USD 8. It produces Keep, Refine, or invalid-execution once. A valid failure
stops factual scaling; a pass prepares, but does not authorize, the sealed
10,000-case evaluation. Paid execution requires a separately frozen
authorization checkpoint.
