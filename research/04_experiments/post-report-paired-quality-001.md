# Post-report paired quality development 001

Prospective plan, 2026-09-08. Run ID:
`post-report-paired-quality-001-live-001`. This is a new exposed synthetic
development packet using the existing bounded paired-pedagogy runner, not a
rerun of sealed data or a replacement of historical results.

Decision: does the existing compact typed V10 composition improve a student's
initial and follow-up experience over V4 on new course-shaped examples?
Prediction: V10 makes the requested next step clearer while preserving source
support and the approved instructional stage. No superiority is presumed.

Fixed before execution: four synthetic courses, three scenarios per course,
Socratic and explanation-first profiles, two student turns per context.
Twenty-four contexts produce 48 paired histories and 96 planned turns.
Each course publishes all three of its source cards, not one selected chunk
per question. Runtime restarts between turns. Source texts contain no real
student/professor information and are approved for these development calls.

Use existing `run_paired_pedagogy_development`, candidate `v10`, default seed
7801, one repetition, model and 3000-token cap as pinned by that runner.
Maximum 320 calls and US$8 conservative reservation, including failures,
within the user's total US$30 authorization. Do not resume by resetting the
budget or overwrite an existing output directory. The remaining US$22 is
unallocated until this run's results justify a further experiment.

Review every response for correctness/support, requested completeness,
relevance, teaching-stage compliance, useful next action, boundary behavior,
and repeated/generic wording. Severity: critical for private disclosure or an
unsupported guarantee; major for wrong rule/application, omitted requested
answer or premature solution; minor for awkward but understandable wording.
Inspect semantic claims in questions and feedback too. Correct citation
identity alone is not a semantic pass. Record uncertain judgments explicitly.

Hard gates: no cross-course citation or private disclosure; zero critical
semantic defects; all planned histories complete; unchanged source snapshot.
Existing 95% content and profile-compliance targets remain diagnostic targets;
this small dependent development sample cannot establish qualification.
Report counts and denominators by course/profile/stage, per-history paired
differences, latency/calls/cost and failures. Keep individual outputs and all
unfavorable results. No product promotion on assistant review alone; prepare
an independently reviewable packet and retain the incumbent fallback.

The required independent human review has not occurred. No professor fidelity,
real-user usability or learning-effect claim follows from this experiment.
