# Meaningful teaching continuation: prospective V4/V5 comparison

Status: design frozen before V5 outputs. No provider calls are made by this specification. The parent runner must record its own bounded execution authorization, model/caps, snapshot and exact commands before any live evaluation.

## Decision and hypothesis

Does a typed, source-linked instructional-move candidate improve meaningful teaching over V4's extractive explanation and generic elicitation while preserving source, privacy and graded-work boundaries? Prediction: V5 can ask a topic-specific starting question, recognize an attempted explanation and offer a concrete next step; generated paraphrases and feedback may introduce unsupported inferences, so source membership alone is not an acceptance criterion.

Control: unchanged opt-in V4 named-referent/bounded-contract composition. Candidate: V5 typed instructional questions, explanatory steps and attempt feedback behind an explicit option; no default release replacement. Hold source sets, student stimuli, professor profiles, model snapshot and budget policy fixed within paired contexts. Histories must be generated by the actual service in each arm. Later histories can diverge; this is a composition comparison, not identical-context single-call inference.

## Data and exposure

Development packet:48 distinct contexts, four authored fictional course domains ×12 forms. Each domain contains two short source concepts. Forms cover direct explanation, initial Socratic elicitation, correct attempt, incorrect attempt, stuck application, repeated difficulty, topic change, two-source explanation, mixed supported/absent question, privacy, graded work, and ambiguous reference. Every context has a prespecified target turn and explicit semantic requirements. All preceding turns are retained and assessed for disclosure/boundary violations. The unit is a context/history, not an individual turn.

Confirmation packet:48 separate contexts with different fictional names, numerical rules and source facts. It reuses the twelve evaluation forms, so it is a source/question holdout, not an independent template or real-course sample. The packet author necessarily sees its labels; runtime implementers and the development judge must not open or execute it until the candidate, development decision and evaluation procedure are frozen. The confirmation data are not used to tune V5. Failed confirmation requires a new recorded decision; do not quietly repair and rerun it.

The four fictional domains do not satisfy the repository's approved heterogeneous real-course evaluation requirement. Existing permissioned course assets remain available; representative final-candidate reevaluation is a separate gap. All current materials are fictional and contain no real instructor/student content. Labels and calibration anchors are assistant-authored, not independent human gold.

## Evaluation criteria fixed before outputs

Use the companion meaningful-continuation rubric. Evaluate factual support, question-specific adequacy, stage-appropriate teaching move, use of the actual student attempt, and safety separately. A quotation, a question mark, an `answer` action, successful transport, or a `Hint:` label is insufficient. A supported numerical application must state the actual correct next action/result. A correct attempt needs recognition or an explicit productive extension; a wrong attempt needs a specific correction/contrast, not a generic request to restate it. A first Socratic response must withhold the full answer and ask a relevant starting question. Required criteria are evaluated against meanings, not mandatory phrases.

Each target receives adequate / defective / uncertain for applicable axes, with short evidence excerpts and rationale. An overall adequate target requires all applicable required axes adequate and no critical violation in the preceding history. Uncertain remains unqualified; do not coerce it to success. Report valid judgments and missing judgments explicitly. Mechanical provenance/transport metrics stay separate. Assistant review may be unblinded; record initial case judgments before inspecting aggregate arm comparisons and disclose exposure rather than calling it independent human review. A flawed old external judge is not reused as authority.

Calibration anchors include supported paraphrases, useful questions and correct feedback, paired with unsupported claims, answer leakage, generic looping and boundary failures. All are retained; they clarify rubric interpretation and are not a natural-language accuracy benchmark. Any future model reviewer requires a separately recorded calibration/transfer audit.

## Comparison, gates and quantity rationale

Report paired target outcomes and case-level failures for all48 development contexts; give each of the12 forms and four courses separately. Use paired context differences, cluster sensitivity by course and leave-one-course-out analysis. Only four source clusters are available: avoid a confirmatory bootstrap or binomial claim treating all turns as independent. Explain the sample as deliberate coverage (4×12), not arbitrary power for population accuracy.

Development progression criterion: at least one clear improvement in each relevant instructional slice (initial elicitation, correct-attempt response, incorrect-attempt response, and stuck application), no new verified critical unsupported/privacy/graded/premature-answer violation, and positive overall paired adequate-minus-defective direction. A single observed critical violation blocks quality promotion regardless of averages. These are engineering progression gates, not the report's human semantic-accuracy threshold. Preserve all failures and ambiguous judgments. If development supports progression, execute confirmation once with unchanged candidate and rubric, reporting all48 contexts. Confirmation cannot establish learning gain, authentic professor imitation, or representative real-course quality.

Record actual calls, tokens/cost, timeouts, p50/p95 latency, history/target count, source/profile/configuration hashes, code revision/dirty state and exact source archive. No missing trial may be omitted. Seeds may shuffle order; they do not imply deterministic provider sampling. Repeated calls are optional prospective robustness checks, not substitutes for distinct contexts. No model/provider/human data are accessed in packet validation.

### Numeric gate binding before outputs

The companion rubric fixes whole-case gates before execution:39/48 useful overall,13/16 primary instructional contexts, zero verified critical violations, and development net definite paired wins≥4 with a win in each primary form. Confirmation uses the same coverage and zero-critical thresholds with nonnegative net definite wins. These are coverage-based engineering targets, not power calculations. Uncertain judgments cannot be used as candidate wins or counted as useful. This numeric binding supersedes the qualitative progression shorthand above without changing any observed outcome (no candidate output has been inspected).

After a successful development/confirmation decision, representative follow-up should select approved, version-bound materials from the existing course portfolio, freeze distinct source/question families and professor-approved style expectations, verify permission scope for the chosen provider, and perform human review on the prespecified subset. Do not infer that permissions are absent or that the fictional packet meets this requirement. No participant recruitment or instructor communication is performed by this plan.
