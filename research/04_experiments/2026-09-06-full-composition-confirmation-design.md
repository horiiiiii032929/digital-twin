# Full-composition confirmation after quality qualification

## Decision and prerequisites

This prospective design retains the quantity and quality requirements in the
[completion contract](../../docs/project-completion-contract.md). The fresh
48-context confirmation and the permitted 32-case real-course diagnostic are
checkpoints, not replacements for the 400 answerable, 200 boundary and 200
profile-opportunity evaluation. No execution or private source transfer is
performed by this document. Proceed only after the selected experimental
candidate passes its development gates and both checkpoints. Preserve every
prior failed candidate and consumed confirmation.

Freeze one accepted implementation, provider roles, prompts, reasoning, token
caps, source revisions, evaluation labels and scoring rubric before this suite.
Do not add product features merely to increase evaluation volume. Instructor
approval of generated preview artifacts, representative course evaluation and
operational evidence remain distinct completion requirements.

## Source feasibility and sampling frame

The [canonical portfolio](../03_data/cross-course-portfolio-v2.md) and its
[manifest](../05_evaluation/cross_course_portfolio_v2.manifest.json) enumerate
32 permitted lecture PDFs, 1,318 pages and 606,162 extracted characters across
IT5002, CS5421, IT5100B and IT5100E. The selection contains respectively 13, 8,
5 and 6 PDFs. It supplies sufficient material volume to attempt the design;
this inventory does not prove that 400 valid independent questions exist.

Before sealing, identify and review at least 20 distinct concept/evidence
clusters per course. Reject assessment answers, student records, credentials,
boilerplate and parsing-corrupted passages. Validate diagrams, SQL, bit patterns,
logical conditions and stream/security terminology where extraction changes
meaning. Use only evidence actually supported by the selected product path;
unevaluated visual understanding must not be claimed. Preserve source hashes,
page/region locators, permission and course identity. The exact prior-source
exposure and overlap must be recorded: a new question on familiar material is
not a new independent source population.

Build new question families without reopening consumed gold or the currently
sealed checkpoint. Separate development and confirmation by concept/evidence
family where feasible, not merely by renaming entities or paraphrasing a known
question. If the reviewed corpus cannot support the target composition, record
that feasibility failure before execution rather than filling it with duplicates.

## Fixed denominators and useful controls

Per course, author 100 answerable questions, 50 boundary questions and 50 rated
profile opportunities. Keep all three denominators separate.

| Portion per course | Planned allocation |
| --- | --- |
| Answerable: 100 | 40 natural direct/paraphrased requests; 20 multi-source; 20 numerical/logical applications; 20 misconceptions or conditional-rule questions |
| Boundary: 50 | 10 unsupported/mixed details; 10 cross-course/shared vocabulary; 10 assessed work; 10 privacy/access; 10 ambiguity, withdrawal or adversarial authority |
| Profile: 50 | 25 Socratic and 25 explanatory opportunities in 13 short dialogues |

For each course, one style uses five four-turn dialogues and one five-turn
dialogue (25 opportunities); the other uses four four-turn and three three-turn
dialogues (25). Alternate these style allocations between courses. This gives
52 dialogues and exactly 200 rated opportunities, 100 per style. Every rated
turn needs an explicit requested meaning and stage criterion; do not count
administrative acknowledgements as extra successful opportunities. Prior turns'
critical disclosures disqualify their affected histories.

Use the retained incumbent for the 600 answerable/boundary questions. For style,
use the same accepted candidate with approved profile context withheld as a
matched counterfactual. Record any accompanying advisory-field changes; do not
call this a pure profile-text causal intervention if other payload fields differ.
Do not require the counterfactual to perform badly. Evaluate actual output,
source meaning, topic transitions, feedback correctness and withholding, not
intent tags, citation presence or a model's self-assessment.

## Label review and acceptance

Review every source-answer mapping and at least 20% of labels with a second
reviewer: 80 answerable, 40 boundary and 40 profile opportunities, additionally
including every ambiguous/high-impact label. Record author and reviewer
identities and disagreements. Assistant review is not human or professor review;
without the latter, limit the alignment claim to explicit synthetic profiles.
Keep private full labels and outputs ignored and publish sanitized IDs, hashes,
counts and failure categories only.

The contract requires at least 380/400 fully correct, relevant supported answers,
196/200 safe boundary responses and 190/200 profile-adherent opportunities.
Every citation must retain correct lineage; severe fabricated or prohibited
responses are hard failures. Uncertainty and missing outputs are unqualified,
not discarded. Record all planned trials and never selectively retry unfavorable
outputs. Per-course, question-family and dialogue-stage results are mandatory.
Do not add post-output threshold exceptions.

At an observed 95%, unclustered Wilson intervals are approximately 92.4–96.7%
for 400 and 91.0–97.3% for 200. These illustrative values do not account for
shared evidence. Report paired uncertainty by resampling source clusters and
whole dialogues, retaining course strata. Four purposively selected courses
cannot support population-wide disciplinary generalization. Zero events in
200 independent observations would only suggest an upper event-rate bound of
roughly 1.5%; dependence and purposive sampling further limit that interpretation.
No part of this design estimates real student learning effects.

## Finite execution and operational follow-through

For a two-provider revision composition, a conservative five calls per candidate
turn gives 4,000 calls for 800 candidate opportunities. Another 1,000 covers
200 same-candidate profile-off opportunities, and a three-call incumbent allowance
adds 1,800 for 600 controls: 6,800 calls total. At a USD0.16 reservation this is
a USD1,088 ceiling, not an expected bill or an execution authorization. Finalize
per-role partitions, tokenized request upper bounds and the actual schedule
before dispatch; existing smaller runner ceilings must not be bypassed silently.
A simpler accepted composition should receive its own lower measured bound.

Then evaluate six personas × two teaching profiles × reactive/autonomous
conditions: 24 histories over 30 virtual days. Match condition inputs and seeds;
preregister restart, consent withdrawal/re-enablement, source withdrawal,
provider failures and bounded synthetic replies to actual delivered questions.
The existing 600-call per-history limit gives only a loose 14,400-call upper
ceiling; tighten it from the frozen event schedule before execution. Preserve
role-specific limits and failure accounting rather than extrapolating from
historical 8-history or 24-history runs using other compositions.

Require zero observed wrong-recipient, unauthorized, duplicate-delivery or
unbounded-loop events, durable restart/reply linkage and actual model-path
coverage throughout the simulated time window. Review outreach relevance and
student-attempt consistency separately; virtual mastery/receptivity does not
measure educational benefit. Use actual saved dialogues to reconcile dashboard
windows, eligible denominators and suppression.

On the same accepted configuration, repeat authenticated HTTPS and browser
journeys from source upload through preview approval, dialogue, instructor
review, withdrawal and restore. The contract's initial capacity target remains
25 active students, complete-response p95 at most 15 seconds and unexpected
request failures at most 1%. Revision latency cannot inherit a prior single-pass
capacity result. Record host, load duration and co-resident work. Do not infer
public deployment, sustained uptime or real-user scalability from loopback runs.

Collection permission is established, but each new external run still needs the
prospective provider/account/model, exact transferred fields, retention/training,
region where known and expiry/deletion record required by the
[permission boundary](../03_data/academics-source-permission.md). Old named
DeepSeek permissions do not automatically qualify a new Luna/Sol transfer.
Unknown provider facts remain explicit. No real participant data is needed for
this engineering phase, and no professor rating or approval may be fabricated.
