# Final-response audit contract and profile refinement

## Decision and provenance

Outcome-informed follow-up to `final-response-support-audit-001-exposed-live-001`.
The original run is stopped, not retried or relabelled: 54 calls, two completed
HTTP responses rejected by cross-field schema checks, 20 inputs reached and 92
blocked. C1/C2 both missed the Nelwick unsupported converse; C2 accepted five
repairs that removed required direct answers. The authored fresh128 bank has had
no auditor calls. Preserve its bytes and pre-output labels.

Question: can explicit response-contract instructions and profile-conditional
teaching obligations remove these concrete failures without weakening validation?
Prediction: fewer contract failures and preserved direct explanations; improved
support detection remains an empirical hypothesis, not an assumed consequence.

## Candidate and control

Keep the original v1 helper behaviour selectable and immutable by default. Add an
explicit experimental `v2` prompt version to the helper and runner, with distinct
task IDs and recorded configuration. Same Sol-high audit, Sol-medium repair,
3000-token cap, public context, typed schemas, shared stopping and one repair
limit. No product selection, deployment or release-profile change.

Only three instruction changes are admitted:

1. State the exact support verdict/issue-code consistency table and keep factual
   support issues separate from teaching-policy dimensions. Do not relax validators,
   reinterpret invalid outputs or automatically retry malformed responses.
2. Make initial-attempt requirements conditional on the authoritative profile and
   actual assessed-work context. A new concept alone does not require a Socratic
   question. Explicit direct-explanation profiles permit the requested supported
   explanation before optional checks. Do not infer assessed status from a normal
   application question. Actual privacy, assessed-work and explicitly unconditional
   attempt restrictions continue to bind. The exposed profiles' integrity sentence
   is terse; assess the complete profile, not a keyword or a universal override.
3. Make the support audit's existing all-claims obligation concrete: a correct
   required conclusion does not license an optional converse, alternative outcome,
   role exclusion or unit conversion. A necessary prerequisite does not establish
   sufficient success unless the source supplies that direction. No model-authored
   confidence or source-ID presence can substitute for support.

The combined v2 intervention does not isolate causal contributions of its three
instruction changes. It is a bounded refinement of a rejected component, not a
claim that verifier redundancy guarantees truth. Do not expose gold or prior
review diagnoses to model calls.
