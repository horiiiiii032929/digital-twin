# Paired pedagogy comparison instrument

Prospective instrument design, before implementation. Paid dispatch requires the
parent's frozen packet/configuration decision; this document is not a release
selection or a live authorization by itself.

Decision question: does the forthcoming v5 pedagogy candidate improve explicitly
reviewed response content over v4 on paired fixed histories while preserving
source, privacy, attempt and integrity boundaries? Prediction and semantic gates
are defined by the separately versioned packet/rubric before dispatch. The v4
control remains available; no automatic release promotion follows this runner.

The instrument accepts synthetic approved concept cards, approved profile values,
a fixed initial question and explicit subsequent student turns per case. Each
arm gets a fresh independent persistent runtime with identical inputs; it restarts
once before the second turn, and records actual restart counts. Order is seeded
and counterbalanced. Seeds control scheduling only, not provider randomness.

All per-turn student text, entire tutor output, response trace, provider call
attribution, latency, usage/cost, exceptions and skipped work remain inspectable.
A packet hash, complete source snapshot, revision and dirty state identify each
run. Exceptions do not silently remove cases. Four independent histories maximum;
finite call/dollar ceilings, fixed model/output cap and packet counts are recorded
before dispatch. Budget stop prevents subsequent dispatch. Defaults stay unchanged.

Automatic checks concern execution and provenance only. Answer actions or valid
citations do not establish semantic quality. Independent per-turn rubric review
must distinguish correctness, request coverage, help-level progression, fresh
concept reset, ambiguous/no-evidence/privacy boundaries and failure causes. No
human learning or instructor-fidelity claim follows synthetic histories. Repeated
turns and repetitions are not independent semantic cases.

contexts across4fictional courses and12forms,84student turns perarm:96independent
arm histories and168paired turns. The prospective worst-case allowance is3calls
perturn (perception, generation, existing repair),504calls total withUSD12.60
ledger reservations. The hard bound800calls/USD20 therefore covers the intended
comparison. Each arm receives half the limit. These are ceilings, not spending
targets. Runtime model isLuna, outputcap1500, reasoninglow, provider timeout30s.

The meaningful-continuation rubric is hashed and archived with the sources.
Semantic decision gates are at least39/48useful contexts,13/16primary instructional
contexts,zero critical violations and at least4net definite wins with representation
across required forms, as specified in that rubric. The runner does not calculate
these from action/citation presence. The confirmation packet must not be opened or
submitted until parent-approved candidate freeze and separate dispatch.
