## Decision rule

- `Keep`: all 43 machine-verifiable checks pass, browser smoke has no critical
  defect, exact selectors and hashes match, and no secret/private source or
  unrestricted model output is committed.
- `Refine`: the valid composition fails a workflow, persistence, safety,
  grounding, browser-critical, or rollback check.
- `Invalid`: an environment or harness defect prevents interpretation. Preserve
  the attempt and correct only that demonstrated operational defect.

The known 10,000+1,000 package is not read, rerun, or rescored. No provider call
is required by this operational qualification.
