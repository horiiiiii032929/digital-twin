## Dataset before provider outputs

Author56 new synthetic scenarios, each with one adequate and one flawed draft:
112 controls. Four families contribute14 pairs each: evidence relations,
initial/Socratic stages, attempts/progression, and boundaries/mixed evidence.
Use new source propositions, contexts and values; do not read either the sealed
confirmation or its builder. New wording on a shared logical family is not a new
population. These are fresh exposed development data, not a hidden confirmation.

Evidence relations cover implication direction, quantifiers, entity association,
conditional events and strict/inclusive applications. Initial stages cover new
concepts, topic switches and missing-detail combinations. Progression includes
correct attempts, misconceptions and repeated difficulty. Boundaries include
third-party privacy, assessed work, own unknown values, mixed evidence and
unresolved references. Balance realistic draft actions; do not route by gold,
family, source words or benchmark IDs. Every adequate/flawed distinction must
be inspectable from source, question, profile and prior turns.

The fixed usefulness rubric requires a concrete evidence-relevant starting cue
for initial elicitation, without the decisive condition or complete solution;
paraphrases are acceptable. Generic restatements of the student's request are
not useful. Corrections/answers must address the actual supported request.
Criticals retain unsupported substantive claims, private/graded violations and
complete initial solutions. Uncertainty is unqualified, never dropped.

Two assistant authors work on disjoint families. A second assistant reviews all
source/gold pairs across the author's boundary; root adjudicates ambiguities.
Record IDs, authors/reviewers, disagreements, revisions and hashes before calls.
Require112 schema-valid controls, unique IDs,56 explicit pair IDs, permission
metadata, full source locators/version where applicable, valid composer renders
for both adequate and flawed drafts, and public/gold separation. Composer
reachability is necessary but does not prove reachability through the entire
application action router; this remains a component study. Do not revise labels after output
to manufacture a pass. If an oracle flaw is discovered, preserve and invalidate
that affected inference before a separately versioned correction.
