# Evaluator calibration and clustered development analysis

Status: prospective specification before the new diagnostic execution; existing underlying tutor outputs are already observed development evidence. No new provider calls or product changes.

Decision question: how much confidence can the current exact-span scorer and repeated simulation counts support, and can explicit diagnostics prevent their misuse as semantic quality evidence?

Prediction: the exact-span baseline will detect malformed provenance and missing required text but accept some substantive defects. A conservative extraction-residue advisory check will flag extra prose without claiming semantic judgment. Cluster-aware summaries will expose heterogeneity hidden by pooled counts.

Baseline: unchanged `score_case` from the four-course development scorer; immutable recorded mechanical scores from live005. Candidate: an advisory `needs_review` diagnostic for extra unquoted content, with no replacement of original pass/fail scores. Cases: eight transformations of an exact scheduler answer (clean, unsupported addition, contradiction addition, missing requirement, wrong source version, malformed citation, empty text, duplicated valid text), plus three Socratic responses (generic, irrelevant, paraphrased solution). The source and question packet remains synthetic development material. Researcher-authored defect labels are not independent human labels. Expected failure coverage and false reassurance are reported by mutation, not as an estimate of natural-language accuracy.

Uncertainty: preserve live005 paired44 outcomes. Report paired discordance, descriptive Wilson intervals (explicit independence approximation), each course difference, leave-one-course-out sensitivity, and seeded percentile bootstrap resampling whole course clusters (4clusters; exploratory/unstable, not confirmatory). Use 10,000 draws, seed620906. Do not infer cross-discipline generalization from four authored mini-courses. For the full operating simulation, pair reactive/autonomous histories by persona and profile (12pairs); report per-history guarded-failure fractions, paired mean differences and resampling of whole pairs. Do not count484turns or720simulated person-days as independent subjects or real learning observations.

Acceptance: malformed source/version/citation and missing/empty answer mutations must fail the baseline; supported exact answer must pass. Advisory must flag unsupported/contradictory extra prose and leave clean extraction unflagged. Generic-question and paraphrased-leakage limitations must remain explicitly visible. Tests must reject unmatched pairs, duplicated cases, nonfinite values, and invalid counts. Statistical implementation uses independent small enumerations/constant-cluster invariants, not assertions copied from a single observed output.

Record all favorable and unfavorable calibration outcomes in one named no-network diagnostic, including code/dirty state, input hashes, per-case outcomes, limitations, and Refine/Keep decision. Neither a bootstrap interval nor passing adversarial fixtures promotes tutor semantic quality. No hidden gold is sent to a tutor or provider; no provider cost or new participant data.
