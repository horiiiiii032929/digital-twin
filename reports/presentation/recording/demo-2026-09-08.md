# Thirty-day product demonstration

[Play the video](digital-twin-30-day-demo-2026-09-08.mp4): 2:14, 1920×1080,
H.264, 30 fps, English subtitles, no audio or desktop/window chrome.

Two synthetic professors, four synthetic students, two courses. The recording
shows professor A's onboarding interview and publication, student opt-in,
question-free initial support, an actual reply, a student opt-out, professor B's
pause, and continued processing through virtual day 30. Four recorded student
views are combined briefly before the video focuses on readable individual
screens. This is a deterministic product baseline demonstration; it does not
show the separately evaluated external-model candidate or establish teaching
quality, professor fidelity or learning gains.

The source/profile approval fixtures and professor B's publication were prepared
through normal APIs between takes. The subtitle discloses the prepared fixtures;
this is not a claim that every setup click was filmed. Virtual time controls the
autonomy observer and due processing; some UI/operator timestamps use wall time.
No generated reply was replaced with authored success text. Generic follow-ups
remain visible, including the final quality limitation subtitle.

By day30 the inbox counts are A1=12, A2=12, B1=2 and B2=1. B2 receives no further
messages after opting out on day2. B1 receives no further messages after its
professor pauses the course on day3. These are scenario observations, not a
large-sample security or learning evaluation.

[Manifest and hashes](demo-2026-09-08-manifest.json) bind raw takes, source PDFs,
editing decisions and known limits. [Saved day30 state](demo-2026-09-08-state.json)
retains the synthetic actions and inbox results. Earlier takes are preserved in
`output/playwright/post-report-demo*` and document the corrected source-metadata
contamination and recorder-locator failures.

## Reproduction

Start a fresh isolated runtime with
`uv run python -m scripts.start_recording_workspace --api-port 8019 --web-port-base 5184`.
Do not reuse or restart the separate older8018 workspace. Generate source PDFs
with `uv run python -m scripts.prepare_recording_materials`. Use the Playwright
CLI recorder (`scripts/record_autonomous_film.py`) for the ordinary UI actions,
and the guarded recording controls for virtual-day advances. The prepared
checkpoint uses `scripts/prepare_recording_checkpoint.py` and explicit synthetic
accounts. Exact source snippets and clip/action snapshots are retained with the
raw takes. The final edit uses `scripts/edit_autonomous_film.py` and
`output/playwright/post-report-demo-final/edit-plan.json`; optional source crops
are validated as in-bounds16:9 regions. FFmpeg combines the four-account view and
adds subtitles. New captures may have different timings and need a revised edit
plan; this is not a claim of byte-identical browser re-recording.

Reviewed the contact sheet and detailed frames for the first outreach, combined
accounts and paused professor screen. The exported file has no audio stream.
The app received a final9+ unread-badge wording correction after recording; the
video retains the observed earlier badge. Submitted report files are unchanged.
